Who We Are

Engineering Consultancy for Infrastructure

We host a house of qualified Architects, Structural Engineers, Quantity Surveyors, Highway Engineers and drafting hands in our design office.

We provide Engineering Consultancy Services in Roads, Buildings, Bridges and other infrastructure sectors, which includes:

  • Laboratory facilities to support material engineering
  • Assessment of residual life of pavements by Falling Weight Deflectometer (FWD) test
  • Assessment of residual life of buildings & industrial structures by NDT methodologies
  • Topographical survey using Total Station instrument
  • Shaping innovative concepts into architectural designs
  • Structural engineering for concrete and steel structures
  • Expertise in steel-concrete composite design

One-Window Premium Solutions

We provide one-window premium engineering solutions starting from conceptualization up to complete execution of building and industrial shed projects, including PEB structures.

Associated Experts

We also have a good number of associated experts, including experienced architects in the relevant fields, who either help execute projects directly or play an advisory role to complete the job to the client’s satisfaction.
